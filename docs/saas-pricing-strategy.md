## 💭 Psychological Pricing Tips

1. **$9 vs $10**: Always end với 9
2. **3 tiers rule**: Majority sẽ chọn middle tier
3. **Annual discount**: Encourage commitment
4. **Usage-based**: Fair pricing cho heavy users
5. **Free trial**: 14 days full Pro features
